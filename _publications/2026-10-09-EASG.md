excerpt: 'EASG is an Explicit Action-Space Grounding framework that lets pretrained vision-language models directly generate executable robot actions. By projecting a TCP-centered action frame into the wrist camera observation and expressing actions through the VLM’s native text interface, it enables zero-shot manipulation across object positions, categories, and robot embodiments without robot-action pretraining or extra action modules, while supporting direct fine-tuning in the same continuous action space.'
