excerpt: 'GAUGE is a measurement-grounded benchmark for evaluating physical fidelity across simulation engines and video world models. It uses calibrated real-world experiments spanning rigid bodies, textiles, and volumetric soft bodies to compare generalized trajectories and diagnose mechanism-specific simulation gaps. The benchmark also evaluates whether generated videos recover both the correct physical law and its real-world parameters.'
