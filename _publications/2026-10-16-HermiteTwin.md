excerpt: 'HermiteTwin separates deformation and contact in deformable digital twins: a coarse C¹ bicubic Hermite shell models Kirchhoff–Love bending, while dense material-space samples are captured, clamped, and released by the gripper. Effective material and contact parameters are identified with CMA-ES followed by adjoint-based refinement, and a reconstructed Gaussian appearance is transported with the simulated motion. Experiments on real cloth and foam cover reconstruction, resimulation, future prediction, and interactions with new objects.'
